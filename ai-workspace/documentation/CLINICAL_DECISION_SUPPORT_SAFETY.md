# Clinical Decision-Support Safety Protocols

## Scope of Decision Support
- Screening assistance only. Not a definitive medical diagnostic tool.
- A photograph alone CANNOT confirm or exclude oral malignancy or dysplasia.
- Visual inspection CANNOT determine tissue invasion depth or nodal metastasis.
- Scalpel incisional biopsy and histopathological examination remain mandatory for all suspected lesions.

## Discrepancy Protocol
- Discrepancy detected when `modelVisualSite` differs from `healthWorkerSite`.
- Discrepancy warning flagged to reviewing clinician.
