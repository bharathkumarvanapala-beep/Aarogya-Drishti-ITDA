---

## 1. Ethical & Regulatory Boundary
Under the AAROHAN Hackathon 2026 specifications and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) principles:
- **No real patient identifiable information (PII)** or real patient photographs may ever be placed into the codebase, mock data, or test fixtures.
- All patient profiles, telephone numbers, Aadhaar placeholders, village habitations, and clinical assessments are 100% synthetic representations designed to model real frontline workflows.
- All oral photographs used in demonstrations are either openly licensed medical educational photographs or synthetic graphics with appropriate attribution.

## 4. Reset & Simulation Tools
A global simulation controls panel (`DemoControlBar`) will be embedded in development builds, allowing evaluators to:
1. Reset mock state to clean factory baseline.
2. Simulate network drops (Online -> Offline -> Slow 2G).
3. Toggle mock AI failures (500 Internal Error / Service Down simulation).
4. Inject test cases into the offline IndexedDB queue.
